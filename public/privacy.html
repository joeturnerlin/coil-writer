Coil privacy

Last updated 2026-10-04. Coil is a screenplay editor made by Joseph Lin.

Your scripts stay on your device

Scripts, version history, notes and settings are stored locally on your Mac (or in your browser, for the web version). Coil has no accounts and does not upload your scripts to us for storage.

When you use an AI feature

AI features (rewrite, analysis, proofreading, structure and continuity checks) send the text needed for that request to an AI provider: Anthropic, OpenAI or Google, depending on the model you choose. If you entered your own API key, the request goes from the app directly to that provider under your key and that provider's terms. If you use a tester token, the request passes through Coil's server at coil-writer.vercel.app, which adds Coil's key and forwards it to the provider. Coil's server does not store or log the text of your requests. Our hosting provider (Vercel) keeps standard request logs such as time and IP address.

API keys

API keys you enter are stored only on your device and are sent only to the provider they belong to.

No tracking

Coil has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Contact

Questions: use the contact details on Coil's App Store or TestFlight page.
